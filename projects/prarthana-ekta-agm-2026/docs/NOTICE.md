# NOTICE – Annual General Meeting (transcription)

**PRARTHANA-EKTA SRA CO-OP. HSG. SOCIETY**
(Regd. No.: MUM/SRA/HSG./T.C./11248/2007)
Bhatt Lane, Poisar, Kandivali (West), Mumbai – 400 067

Date: 02 September 2026

## NOTICE

Notice is hereby given to all members/Owners of the society that the **ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING** will be held on **27th Sep, 2026 (Sunday) at 10.30 a.m.** at the society premises.
Members are requested to attend the meeting without fail.

The agenda will be as given below:

1. To receive and adopt the Annual Report and Audited Financial Statement for the year 2025-2026.
2. Consider and approve appointment of statutory auditors for the next F.Y. 2026-2027.
3. To discuss revised professional fees for Accounting & society general works.
4. To consider any other issue brought by any member with the permission of the chair.
5. **Rental members are not allowed in this meeting.**
6. Followed by Lunch.
7. Vote of Thanks.

## Note

1. In case there is no quorum for the meeting at the time fixed, the meeting will be adjourned for half an hour and after half an hour the proceedings of the meeting will commence at the same place and members present will form the quorum.
2. In case of any observation on the statement of accounts, members may inform the Secretary in writing at least 2 days before the commencement of the meeting.

For Prarthana Ekta SRA Co-op Housing Society Ltd.
(Society seal)
Chairman / Secretary (signed)
