# Pre-AGM Checklist

## Before 25 Sep 2026 (observation deadline)

- [ ] Obtain copy of Annual Report FY 2025-26
- [ ] Obtain Audited Financial Statement (balance sheet, income & expenditure, receipts & payments)
- [ ] Obtain auditor's report and any qualifications
- [ ] Compare FY 2025-26 expenses with FY 2024-25 for large variances
- [ ] Check maintenance dues, arrears and recovery status
- [ ] Check sinking fund and repair fund balances
- [ ] Note proposed statutory auditor and fee for FY 2026-27
- [ ] Note current vs. proposed accounting / professional fees
- [ ] Draft written observations (OBSERVATION_LETTER.md) and submit to Secretary, keep acknowledged copy

## Day of AGM – 27 Sep 2026

- [ ] Confirm membership status (owner, not rental) – carry share certificate / ownership proof if asked
- [ ] Reach society premises by 10:15 a.m.
- [ ] Sign attendance register
- [ ] Note whether quorum was met at 10:30 a.m. or meeting adjourned to 11:00 a.m.
- [ ] Carry printed observations and the audited accounts
- [ ] Record decisions and votes per agenda item in MINUTES_TEMPLATE.md
- [ ] Request copy of official minutes from the Secretary after the meeting

## After AGM

- [ ] Update AGENDA_TRACKER.md decisions log
- [ ] File official minutes in `docs/` when received
- [ ] Track any resolutions with follow-up dates
